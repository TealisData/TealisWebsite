## Direction contract

THESIS: Every engagement is a journey on two lines, Build (ink) and Teach (brand blue), that start together at the discovery call and meet again at Handover, where the client's team owns the work. Refuses the consulting template: split hero, eyebrow labels, rows of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Transit-diagram grammar on the existing palette: two thick parallel rails (ink + brand blue, round caps, 45° bends only), white station dots with ink rings, interchange pills, station labels in a strict order (name, code, level/length). The Tealis mark is the Handover interchange. Only "Book a call" is a filled control.

STORY: Visitors grasp in one look that the same two people build and teach, see how a project travels and what they get at each stop, meet the founders as stations, and book a real free slot.
